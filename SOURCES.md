# Line Dancer content sources

Catalog links and tutorial identities were checked on November 8, 2026. The catalog is a handpicked collection of dance-floor favorites, not a live popularity chart. Choreography versions vary; each entry identifies its choreographer and links to that version’s reference sheet.

## Dances and lessons

- **A Bar Song — Ben Murphy.** Beginner, 32 counts, 4 walls. [CopperKnob sheet](https://www.copperknob.co.uk/stepsheets/9ZRL2CY/a-bar-song), [original PDF](https://www.copperknob.co.uk/stepsheets/9ZRL2CY/a-bar-song/download/pdf), [Dirt Road Dancing tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZwxZKYeJ570).
- **Boot Scootin’ Boogie — Bill Bader.** Beginner, 32 counts, 4 walls; also called Vancouver Boogie. [CopperKnob sheet](https://www.copperknob.co.uk/stepsheets/QGCF4T9/boot-scootin-boogie), [original PDF](https://www.copperknob.co.uk/stepsheets/QGCF4T9/boot-scootin-boogie/download/pdf), [Liz Collett lesson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n37INIBiN8Q). The lesson is also linked by [the choreographer’s website](https://www.billbader.com/billdances_p6.htm).
- **Copperhead Road — Steve Smith.** Absolute Beginner, 24 counts, 4 walls. [CopperKnob sheet](https://www.copperknob.co.uk/stepsheets/9HV6XRP/copperhead-road), [original PDF](https://www.copperknob.co.uk/stepsheets/9HV6XRP/copperhead-road/download/pdf), [Beginner Dance Tutorials lesson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=av13Vxce8Qw).
- **Texas Hold ’Em — Guylaine Bourdages.** Improver, 32 counts, 4 walls. [CopperKnob sheet](https://www.copperknob.co.uk/stepsheets/Y32VV3H/texas-hold-em), [original PDF](https://www.copperknob.co.uk/stepsheets/Y32VV3H/texas-hold-em/download/pdf), [LineDanceDallas5 dance and teach](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OArFg3Uprds).
- **Watermelon Crawl — Sue Lipscomb.** Improver, 40 counts, 4 walls. [CopperKnob sheet](https://www.copperknob.co.uk/stepsheets/85C68JG/watermelon-crawl), [original PDF](https://www.copperknob.co.uk/stepsheets/85C68JG/watermelon-crawl/download/pdf), [Bar SZ Ranch lesson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wAaEIVqsPcY).
- **American Kids — Randy Pelletier.** High Beginner, 32 counts, 4 walls. [CopperKnob sheet](https://www.copperknob.co.uk/stepsheets/ZYQMHP5/american-kids), [original PDF](https://www.copperknob.co.uk/stepsheets/ZYQMHP5/american-kids/download/pdf), [Sherry Barrett full-length teach](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aV6Nj7l4JCA).
- **Shivers — Alison Metelnick and Peter Metelnick.** Intermediate, 64 counts, 4 walls. [CopperKnob sheet](https://www.copperknob.co.uk/stepsheets/55H2SCY/shivers), [original PDF](https://www.copperknob.co.uk/stepsheets/55H2SCY/shivers/download/pdf), [LineDanceDallas3 dance and teach](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vMPtLGA0Ch8). This is distinct from Brandon Zahorsky’s *Gives Me Shivers*.
- **Good Time — Jenny Cain.** Improver, 48 counts, 4 walls. [CopperKnob sheet](https://www.copperknob.co.uk/stepsheets/86SCMVC/good-time), [original PDF](https://www.copperknob.co.uk/stepsheets/86SCMVC/good-time/download/pdf), [official Alan Jackson dance instruction](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rs5f8CYyLBo).

Absolute Beginner and High Beginner are grouped under the app’s Beginner filter. The published level and relevant tag/restart reminders are retained in each dance’s notes. Descriptions, brief reminders, and glossary explanations are original app copy. Full step sheets remain with their publishers and open in their original PDF format. Tutorials are embedded from YouTube with direct links to the creators’ videos.
